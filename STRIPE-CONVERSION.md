## 🎯 Stripe/Linear Design Principles Applied

### 1. Neutral Color Palette ✅
- Primary: `slate-900`
- Text: `slate-900/700/600/500`
- Borders: `slate-200/60`
- Background: `slate-50`

### 2. Solid Colors (No Gradients) ✅
- Buttons: Solid `bg-slate-900`
- Text: Solid colors only
- Borders: Solid slate tones

### 3. Soft Shadows ✅
- Changed from `shadow-2xl` to `shadow-sm`
- Subtle elevation only

### 4. Clean Typography ✅
- `font-bold` for headings
- `font-semibold` for subheadings
- `font-medium` for buttons
- `font-normal` for body

### 5. Minimal Aesthetic ✅
- No bright colors
- No flashy gradients
- Professional and clean
